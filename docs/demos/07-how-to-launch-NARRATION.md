# How to Launch — Ndotonic Author Series

Voice: `en-US-AvaMultilingualNeural` via edge-tts (-2%).

Duration: **2:11** (131.1s)

## 1. How to Launch (8.76s)

A book launch works best when it begins before publication day. Give interested readers a simple way to hear from you and follow the book's progress.

## 2. One Clear Goal (13.82s)

Choose one clear launch goal: early reviews, email signups, sales, or introductions to a new audience. Let that goal guide your actions. A launch that tries to do everything usually does nothing well.

## 3. Prepare Small Assets (15.0s)

Prepare a small set of assets: cover image, concise description, author bio, sample chapter, and a few posts that explain why this book exists. You do not need a huge campaign — you need a clear, repeatable message.

## 4. Warm The Room Early (11.71s)

Warm the room early. Tell friends and early readers. Invite honest feedback. Collect a short interest list so launch day is not a cold start into silence.

## 5. Publish With Confidence (13.66s)

On Ndotonic, publish with confidence: confirm rights, set price and formats, and preview the buyer experience for ebook and audiobook. A clean listing is part of the launch, not an afterthought.

## 6. Invite, Don't Shout (11.93s)

On launch week, invite rather than shout. Share useful context, thank supporters, and ask for honest reviews. People respond to sincerity more reliably than to volume.

## 7. Use Owned Channels (14.18s)

Use owned channels. Email, direct messages, and your presence on Ndotonic matter more than algorithm luck. When it fits, GLC Media can help carry the story to a live audience — but start with people who already care.

## 8. Keep Connecting After Day One (10.42s)

Keep connecting after the first day. Follow up with readers, share useful excerpts, and plan the next conversation. A launch is a beginning, not a finish line.

## 9. Measure What Matters (12.94s)

Measure what matters for the goal you chose — reviews, signups, or sales. Learn which message worked. Adjust the next week. Do not turn a quiet first day into a story about your worth as an author.

## 10. You Are An Author (16.66s)

You started with an idea. You built a draft, a structure, characters, a plot, a polished manuscript, and a promise to readers. That is authorship. Visit ndotonic.com, launch with care, and keep going. Thank you for watching.
