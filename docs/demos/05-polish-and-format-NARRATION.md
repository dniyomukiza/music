# Polish and Format — Ndotonic Author Series

Voice: `en-US-AvaMultilingualNeural` via edge-tts (-2%).

Duration: **2:03** (122.7s)

## 1. Polish and Format (8.47s)

Polishing comes after drafting. First make the book complete; then edit in passes so you can see the big picture before fixing commas.

## 2. Structure Pass (12.53s)

Read for structure first: missing explanations, repeated ideas, slow sections, and chapters that do not serve the reader's journey. Fix order and gaps before you polish sentences.

## 3. Clarity Pass (13.39s)

Next, edit for clarity. Prefer concrete words, short clean sentences, and examples that make an unfamiliar idea easy to understand. If a sentence must be read twice, rewrite it once.

## 4. Voice And Consistency (13.37s)

Check consistency: names, timeline, facts, tone, and point of view. A reader trusts a book that feels like one mind from start to finish. Keep a running list of decisions and stick to them.

## 5. Line Edit Lightly (10.73s)

Then line-edit lightly. Cut filler phrases, vary sentence length, and keep only words that earn their place. Do not chase perfection forever — chase readability.

## 6. Format Consistently (13.97s)

Format consistently: styles for headings and body text, page breaks where chapters start, and consistent spacing and emphasis. Clean hierarchy helps readers navigate and helps publishing tools behave.

## 7. Proof On A Real Device (14.33s)

Proof on a real device or printout. Read aloud once. On this pass, fix only true errors — typos, broken formatting, missing words. Do not reopen big structure unless something is broken.

## 8. AI Help, Human Judgment (13.15s)

In Ndotonic, AI editing modes can surface grammar issues and craft notes. Treat them as suggestions. Accept only what fits your voice and intent. You remain the author of record.

## 9. Know When To Stop (11.5s)

Know when to stop. Set a polish deadline. Ship the complete book. You can revise future editions. Endless polishing is often fear wearing a productive costume.

## 10. Your Next Step (9.24s)

Your next step: do one structure pass and mark gaps, then rewrite your foggiest page for clarity. Next up: cover and description.
