# GLC Media TV programs

Locked slate for the linear channel. Social and the website take cuts from these shows. Do not merge the formats.

| Slot | Show | Cadence | Clock |
|---|---|---|---|
| Live block | News | Daily | Short bulletin, then repeats |
| Mid-clock | GLC Compare | 2–3 times a week | 8–12 min |
| Evening | Interviews | Weekly | 8–15 min |
| Evening / weekend | Founder journey | Weekly or monthly | 8–15 min |
| Social | 15s host clip from any show | Daily | Hook only |

X and YouTube get the hook plus the full cut. The website gets the Compare write-up. TV gets the repeating series with GLC Media on the set.

---

## News

Flagship. Do not replace this with comparison videos or interviews.

- **Search:** xAI / Grok search (`x_search`) for what people are saying now.
- **Picture and video:** Grok Imagine (host still + presenter clip). GLC Media branding stays in the background.
- **Job on TV:** near-live bulletin. Names, argument, face on camera.
- **Cuts:** 15s Imagine clip for X. Full bulletin on TV and YouTube.

Keep News as news. Do not turn every X trend into an hour.

---

## GLC Compare

Recurring desk show. One fight per episode: two models, two agents, two MCP setups, two plans.

Grok Bot runs both sides live and scores three things only:

1. Usability
2. Efficiency
3. Price

Then a winner on screen.

- **Production:** Grok Bot demo, same host and clock each episode.
- **TV / YouTube:** the 8–12 min desk cut.
- **X:** 15s hook (who won, on what).
- **Website:** the full comparison write-up (usability, efficiency, prices, verdict).

A thread alone is not TV.

---

## Interviews

Conversations with entrepreneurs, startups, and businesses.

- **Who:** founders and operators with a real product, not a pitch deck.
- **Job on TV:** one guest, one story, one thing they shipped.
- **Cuts:** full interview on TV and YouTube. One 15s quote for X.

This is a weekly original, not a daily filler.

---

## Founder journey

Memoir / documentary. One person, one path, 8–15 minutes.

- **Cadence:** weekly or monthly. Not daily.
- **Job:** brand and trust. It will not fill the 24/7 clock.
- **Cuts:** full piece on TV and YouTube. One still or 15s clip for X.

Closer to a story film than to News.

## Do not mix

- News stays News. Search + Imagine. Not a review show.
- Compare stays a desk show. Bot demo + three scores + website post.
- Interviews stay guest-led. Not a founder montage.
- Founder journey stays a story. Not a news bulletin.
