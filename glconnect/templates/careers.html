Board Member

Leadership

Join GLC’s board to guide strategy, governance, and growth as we build a platform where creators connect with their audiences through music, storytelling, and publishing tools.

What You'll Be Doing

  • Advise on company vision, product direction, and long-term positioning in the creator economy.
  • Support governance, risk oversight, and ethical standards as the platform scales.
  • Help shape partnerships, fundraising, and go-to-market strategy with founders and leadership.
  • Bring an independent perspective on finance, operations, technology, media, or community building as relevant to your background.
  • Participate in periodic board meetings and strategic reviews (remote-friendly).

About You

  • Senior experience in technology, media, publishing, startups, or nonprofit/cultural leadership.
  • Track record of strategic judgment—scaling products, building teams, or advising early-stage ventures.
  • Genuine interest in empowering independent creators and underserved voices.
  • Ability to commit time for board duties alongside other professional obligations.
  • Integrity, discretion, and a collaborative working style with founders and stakeholders.

Nice-to-haves

  • Prior board or advisory experience at a startup or growth-stage company
  • Network in music, publishing, education, or creator platforms
  • Experience with fundraising, M&A, or international expansion
