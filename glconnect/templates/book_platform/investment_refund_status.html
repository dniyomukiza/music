About Refunds
When are refunds issued?
  • If the author fails to complete the book within 180 days (6 months) after funding
  • If the author completes the book but fails to publish it within 30 days
  • If the campaign is cancelled by the author or platform
Refund Process
  1. Refund request is automatically created when deadlines are missed
  2. Request is reviewed and processed by the platform
  3. Funds are returned to your original payment method
  4. You will receive a confirmation email once processed
Note: Refund processing may take 5-10 business days depending on your payment method.
