YouTube TV (HLS) download

Paste a YouTube video or playlist URL only to download, the live channel never streams YouTube links. Each item becomes a local MP4 under glconnect/static/ytautovid/. After rename, the job writes video/videolist.m3u immediately with /liqfolder/glconnect/static/ytautovid/… paths (plus extras + DB), then reconciles downloaded_videos (insert or fix local_path), then refreshes the M3U again. Sync TV playlist only runs the same merge without downloading. Liquidsoap plays local paths only; it reloads when the M3U changes. Same admin-only, background idea as music downloads, but TV uses a separate playlist and DB table.
