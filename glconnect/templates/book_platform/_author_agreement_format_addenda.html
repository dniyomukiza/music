Format addenda

Print edition. If you enable print sales, you agree to ship physical books to buyers at the address Ink provides, within the handling time you set. You are responsible for print quality, packaging, carrier choice, and delivery. Ink collects the book price and shipping; you fulfill shipment. Ink is not your printer or freight broker.

Audiobook (platform TTS). If you generate or publish an audiobook here, you agree it is produced from your text using platform text-to-speech voices. You will preview the audio before marketplace publish. Quality and voice selection are your responsibility within platform limits.

Book campaigns (patronage). If you run a community funding campaign, you agree supporters are making contributions to help your book exist—not purchasing securities or financial returns. Campaign copy must not promise profit or revenue share to backers.
