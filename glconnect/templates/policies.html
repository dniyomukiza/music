{% if active_policy == 'terms' %}

1. The platform

Ndotonic provides Ink Studio creator tools, a marketplace, book campaigns, and related media features. We are a platform and marketplace—not an author’s employer, literary agent, printer, insurer, tax adviser, or legal adviser.

2. Accounts and acceptable use

Keep account information accurate, protect your credentials, and use the platform lawfully. You may not use the service for fraud, infringement, harassment, deceptive listings, or unlawful disclosure of another person’s private information. We may restrict or suspend accounts when needed to protect users, comply with law, or investigate credible abuse reports.

3. Buyer and creator terms

Buyers purchase formats offered by an author: ebook, audiobook, print, or a permitted combination. Authors who list work accept the separate Author Publishing Agreement and per-title listing attestation. Those terms govern author rights, revenue share, payouts, AI responsibilities, and takedowns.

4. Changes and contact

We may update these policies when the service or law changes. Material changes may require renewed acceptance where applicable. For questions, use Support.

{% elif active_policy == 'privacy' %}

1. Information we collect

We collect information you provide, such as your name, email, username, profile details, content, support messages, purchases, and publishing activity. We also collect technical and security information needed to operate the service, including device/browser data, logs, cookies or similar tools, and fraud-prevention signals.

2. How we use it

We use this information to operate and secure accounts, provide publishing and marketplace features, process purchases and payouts, respond to support requests, prevent misuse, comply with legal obligations, and improve the service. We do not sell personal information as a standalone product.

3. Payments, public profiles, and providers

Stripe processes checkout and author payout details. Ndotonic does not receive or store full card numbers, security codes, or bank login credentials. Public listings may show a pen name, bio, cover, book details, reviews, campaign information, and other material you choose to publish; email addresses are not displayed on public listings. Providers may process data for payments, hosting, email, analytics, and AI features.

4. Your choices

You can update public profile information in your account and ask Support about account closure or access requests. Some records must be retained for completed transactions, fraud prevention, tax, dispute resolution, or other lawful business needs. We will verify requests and respond as applicable law requires.

Do not upload private, sensitive, or personal information about another person unless you have a lawful basis and any required permission to do so.

{% elif active_policy == 'refunds' %}

1. Digital books and audiobooks

Digital formats are delivered or made available immediately after a completed purchase. Because they can be accessed or copied, they are generally not returnable after delivery, except where required by applicable law or where there is a verified technical, duplicate-charge, or unauthorized-payment issue. Contact Support with the order reference and a clear description of the issue.

2. Print orders

Print books are sold by the author and shipped by that author. A buyer may request cancellation through Support before the order is marked shipped; availability depends on whether the author can stop fulfillment. If the order is not shipped by the promised time, the buyer must be offered a meaningful choice between a revised shipment date and cancellation with a prompt refund of the original payment. A refund for a delayed or unfulfilled order is not replaced with store credit unless the buyer freely chooses that option.

3. Defective, incorrect, or lost print books

Contact Support promptly with the order reference, photos where appropriate, and a description of the problem. We will coordinate with the author to assess replacement, reshipment, or refund under applicable law and the facts of the order. This policy does not remove any non-waivable consumer rights.

4. Book campaigns

Campaign contributions are patronage, not investments, and do not grant a share of future sales. Campaign-specific refund rules and milestones are shown before a contribution is made. If a campaign misses its stated goal or is otherwise refunded under its displayed terms, refunds are returned to the original payment method where practicable.

{% elif active_policy == 'shipping' %}

1. Who ships the book

Ink Studio is not a print-on-demand printer. A print listing is fulfilled by its author: Ink collects checkout payment, securely captures the buyer’s shipping address, and makes the fulfillment details available to the author so they can print and ship the book.

2. Shipping promises

Print listings display the author’s handling window and shipping charge before checkout. Authors must have a reasonable basis for that promise and must ship within it. Print shipping is currently limited to US delivery addresses unless the listing clearly states otherwise.

3. Delays, tracking, and cancellation

If an author cannot ship on time, the buyer must receive a delay notice with a revised shipment date and the option to cancel for a prompt refund. When shipped, the author supplies the carrier, tracking number, and expected delivery window. Buyers can view order status in My Library.

4. Address accuracy

Buyers must provide an accurate, deliverable address at checkout. Contact Support as soon as possible if an address needs correction. Once an order is marked shipped, address changes, reshipment costs, and delivery issues depend on the carrier and the author’s ability to help.

{% elif active_policy == 'rights' %}

1. Rights authors must hold

Authors remain responsible for obtaining the rights and permissions needed to publish, sell, distribute, and promote their text, cover art, images, audio, translations, excerpts, and metadata. This includes copyright, trademark, publicity, privacy, and contractual rights. Listing a work does not transfer the author’s copyright to Ndotonic.

2. Limited platform license

Authors give Ndotonic the limited non-exclusive right to host, reproduce, display, market, sell, deliver, and technically process their listed work only as needed to operate the service and the formats the author enables.

3. Reporting a concern

To report alleged infringement, impersonation, privacy misuse, or an unlawful listing, contact Support with the URL or title, your contact information, the rights or policy concern, and enough detail for us to investigate. Do not submit sensitive personal information unless necessary.

4. Review and takedown

We may remove, restrict, or pause a listing while reviewing a credible claim, legal request, or safety issue. We may ask the author and reporter for additional information. Nothing in this policy limits formal legal rights or procedures that apply in a particular jurisdiction.

{% elif active_policy == 'ai' %}

1. Optional tools, human responsibility

Ink Studio may offer optional AI-assisted ideation, editing, cover, narration, and related tools. AI output can be inaccurate, incomplete, offensive, or similar to existing material. Authors—not Ndotonic—choose whether to use output and are responsible for reviewing, editing, and approving anything they publish.

2. Commercial rights and disclosure

Before using AI-assisted text, art, audio, or translations commercially, authors must confirm they have the rights required by the applicable tool provider and by law. Authors must not present AI-generated material as independently verified fact, impersonate a real person, use protected names or likenesses without permission, or make misleading claims about how work was created.

3. Third-party processing

Some features send prompts or content to third-party AI providers. Use those features only if you are permitted to share the material. Do not submit confidential client work, unpublished third-party manuscripts, sensitive personal data, or material you do not have authority to process.

4. Enforcement

We may restrict AI features or remove AI-assisted content when there is a credible rights, safety, deception, or policy concern. Authors must cooperate with reasonable requests related to a complaint or takedown.
